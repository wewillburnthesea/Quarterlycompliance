The law panels

Regulation wording is reproduced for study from the SEBI regulations as amended up to late 2025, plus later exchange FAQs where noted. Words in [square brackets] are editorial. Confirm against SEBI's official text and the latest exchange circulars.

The dates

Working-day counts skip weekends and BSE trading holidays (2026 list built in; add 2027 when BSE publishes it). Calendar-day deadlines that land on a holiday are flagged so you can file early.

Shared progress

Status, filing dates and acknowledgement numbers are shared with everyone who can open this page, with who changed what and when. Quiz answers stay private to you. A training aid, not legal advice.
